### **Handling** *(v.)*
* **Pronunciación:** Ján-dlin
* **Significado:** Managing, dealing with, or physically touching something.
* **Ejemplo:** *He is handling the customer complaints professionally.*
* **Español:** Manejando, gestionando o manipulando.

* **Pronunciación:** éi-bol
* **Significado:** Having the power, skill, or opportunity to do something.
* **Ejemplo:** *She is able to speak three languages fluently.*
* **Español:** Capaz.
